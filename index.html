OPERATIONAL WORKFLOW

Five-Step Precision Flight Workflow

Every mission is executed through 5 structured phases ensuring cinematic excellence, legal airspace compliance, and absolute safety.

01

Project Consultation & Vision

Aligning on visual concepts, deliverables, aspect ratios, style references, and shooting schedules with your creative team.

02

Airspace Assessment (SIDOPI GO)

Screening coordinate locations against airport KKOP zones, 120m AGL altitude caps, and topography hazard assessment.

03

Permits & Safety Risk Assessment

Securing airspace authorizations when needed, pre-flight safety checklists, and establishing emergency fail-safe landing zones.

04

Precision Flight Execution

On-site flight operations by Certified Pilot featuring smooth camera gimbal moves, live monitoring, and calibrated battery management.

05

Post-Production & Master Delivery

Raw log file integrity inspection, professional D-Log M color grading, master 5.1K/4K exports, and fast cloud delivery.
